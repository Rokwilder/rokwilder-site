Refund policy

These are Rokwilder Scouting's own refund rules, written in plain English. They are not legal advice. Nothing is for sale yet and checkout is not open. Last updated November 9, 2026.

Short version: cancel the Full Pass anytime, online. If something we made for you is wrong, we fix it, and if we can't, we refund it. Boots-on-the-Ground can be refunded within 72 hours of buying, if we haven't started traveling or scouting for it. Nothing is for sale yet, so no one has been charged.

You agree to this policy when you check the box at checkout. It's part of our terms of use.

UnitIQ Full Pass

  • Cancel anytime online from My account ("Manage billing or cancel"), or by email. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the year you already paid for.
  • If you ask within 7 days of your first purchase or of a renewal charge, we'll refund that charge in full. This is once per person.
  • After 7 days, we don't give partial refunds for the unused part of the year.
  • If we stop offering the Full Pass before your paid year ends, we refund the unused part.
  • A Full Pass that comes with a package is part of that package and isn't refunded on its own.

Founding Hunter price

The Founding Hunter price ($19.99 a year for the first 200 members) stays locked only as long as your subscription keeps renewing without a break. If you cancel, get a refund, or let it lapse, the founder price ends, and coming back later means paying the regular price at that time.

UnitIQ Scouting Package and extra units

These are custom digital products made for your unit, species and season. The package includes 1 free revision. The refund rules below are the same.

  • Before we deliver it: cancel for a full refund.
  • If it sells out after you pay: full refund.
  • After we deliver it: no refund, because digital maps and files can't be returned. But if we got something wrong, like the wrong unit or species, a broken or missing file, or a mistake in our maps, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll fix it and re-send it. If we can't fix it, we'll refund it.

Boots-on-the-Ground

In-person scouting means real travel, fuel and time out of our pocket, so the refund window is short.

  • Within 72 hours of buying: full refund, as long as we haven't started traveling or scouting for your hunt.
  • After that: no refund, except if we cancel or weather, fire or a closure stops the scout (see below).
  • Once we've started traveling or scouting for your hunt: you still get the field report and waypoints for whatever we scouted.
  • If we cancel before we start: you choose a full refund or a new date.
  • If weather, fire or a closure stops the scout: before we start traveling, you choose a full refund or a new date. After we've started traveling, you choose a new date or the field report for what we scouted, but there's no cash refund.

What isn't a reason for a refund

We can't refund because you didn't draw a tag, didn't see or harvest an animal, had bad weather or closures during your own hunt, changed your plans, or a draw odds estimate didn't match the draw. Our maps and spots are estimates, not guarantees.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, including any sales tax you paid. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

How to ask

Email support@rokwilder.com from the email on your account. Tell us what you bought and what's going on. We'll answer as soon as we can.

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; it's usually faster to fix it together. If a charge is disputed, we'll give the bank our records of the order, delivery and this policy.

Nothing in this policy takes away any rights you have under the law.
