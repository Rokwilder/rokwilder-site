Refund policy

Draft. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer or your tax/business pro review before launch. This page is a plain-English draft and may change. Nothing is for sale yet and checkout is not open. Items marked DRAFT are choices still being confirmed. Last updated November 3, 2026.

Short version: cancel the Full Pass anytime. If something we made for you is wrong, we fix it. In-person scouting can be cancelled with notice before we start traveling for it. Nothing is for sale yet, so no one has been charged.

UnitIQ Full Pass DRAFT

  • You can cancel anytime to stop the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the year you already paid for.
  • If you ask within 7 days of your first purchase or of a renewal charge, and you haven't used the Full Pass much, we'll refund that charge in full.
  • After 7 days, we don't give partial refunds for the unused part of the year.
  • A Full Pass that comes with a package is part of that package and isn't refunded on its own.

Founding Hunter price DRAFT

The Founding Hunter price ($19.99 a year for the first 200 members) stays locked only as long as your subscription keeps renewing without a break. If you cancel, get a refund, or let it lapse, the founder price ends, and coming back later means paying the regular price at that time.

Hotspot Map Pack, Full Scouting Package and extra units DRAFT

These are custom digital products made for your unit and species.

  • Before we deliver it: cancel for a full refund.
  • After we deliver it: no refund, because the maps and files can't be returned. But if we got something wrong, like the wrong unit or species, a broken or missing file, or a mistake in our maps, tell us and we'll fix it and re-send it.

Boots-on-the-Ground DRAFT

In-person scouting means real travel, fuel and time out of our pocket, so refunds depend on whether we've started.

  • Before we start traveling or scouting for your hunt:
    • 14 or more days before the scheduled scout date: full refund.
    • 7 to 13 days before: 50% refund.
    • Less than 7 days before: no refund.
  • Once we've started traveling or scouting for your hunt: no refund, no matter how many days are left before the scout date. You still get the field report and waypoints for whatever we scouted.
  • If we cancel before we start: you choose a full refund or a new date.
  • If weather, fire or a closure stops the scout: before we start traveling, you choose a full refund or a new date. After we've started traveling, you choose a new date or the field report for what we scouted, but there's no cash refund.

How refunds are paid DRAFT

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

How to ask

Email support@rokwilder.com from the email on your account. Tell us what you bought and what's going on. Nothing in this policy takes away any rights you have under the law.
