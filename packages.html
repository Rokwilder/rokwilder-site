What's in the paper scout?

This is part of the UnitIQ Scouting Package. It is not a separate product. We split the unit into small squares and score each one on what elk or deer need at that time of year: the right elevation for the season, cover (dark timber, thick brush), feed (meadows, parks, open slopes), distance to water, distance from roads and trailheads, and known herd range and migration data from CPW and USGS collar studies. The squares that score best become hotspots.

Think of it as everything the animals want this time of year, far from the road. It's a place to start glassing and hiking. It is not a guarantee animals are there on the day you hunt. You still have to hunt.

Limited: UnitIQ Scouting Packages are limited per unit and per season, so we don't send too many hunters to the same spots. The limit follows the official tag numbers for that unit and species: 1 package for every 25 tags, at least 3 and at most 30 for that unit that season. Over-the-counter and unlimited-tag units are a flat 30 per unit per season. An extra-unit add-on counts toward the cap of that extra unit. In larger units, buyers get a varied mix of spots so the same spots aren't handed to everyone. The Full Pass does not count. If a unit sells out after you pay, we refund that purchase in full.
