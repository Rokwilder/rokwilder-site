How these three lists differ

  • Season windows are not a draw and not a harvest rate. They are the 2026 general-season dates, weapons, and places in the Hunt Planner. The planner's permit field is an unlimited placeholder, so this page does not show a tag count for a season window.
  • Over the counter is not the same as a general season. The 2026 big-game brochure says resident general deer tags and resident elk tags in uncapped zones go on sale over the counter in early July, after controlled-hunt results. Resident capped elk tags went on sale July 8, 2026, and Sawtooth capped elk tags on July 10, 2026. This page does not show how many of those tags are left.
  • Nonresident general deer and elk tags are drawn. The same 2026 brochure says that, beginning with the 2026 season, those tags were issued through a random draw rather than a first-come sale. The draw does not change where the tag is valid. It decides who receives the nonresident tag.
  • 2026 bands are first-choice history. IDFG's draw-odds description says a zone or unit rate is the tags for that one place divided by the people who applied for that place. This page uses the first-choice drawn and applied counts from that description. Every person drawn is "All drew." At least three out of four is "Most drew." At least one out of four is "Some drew." At least one out of twenty is "Few drew." Below that, the row stays off the list. One regular-deer unit sits where IDFG's rounded whole-number rate would cross a line. The band follows the counts. The exact rates are not shown.
  • A later choice is a different result. The 2026 description says every elk tag was awarded on a first or second choice, and every regular and white-tailed deer tag was awarded on a first, second, or third choice. If a card says a later choice also received tags, that is not included in the first-choice band.
  • The statewide sentence is not your zone. IDFG prints one overall elk figure and one overall deer figure for the whole 2026 drawing. Those are not the result for a zone or unit, and they are not shown. Outfitter-allocated tags are left out of IDFG's odds tables, so they are left out here.
  • A deer unit can stop before its own tag number is filled. IDFG says the sum of the unit tag numbers is larger than the statewide nonresident deer limit of 15,500, including outfitter tags, so the statewide limit can be reached first. The band is still the first-choice people who were drawn.
  • DAV is not the same list in both years. The 2026 odds description gives one elk result and one deer result for disabled American veteran tags, not a result by zone or unit, and says DAV elk tags in uncapped zones had no zone limit in that draw. Those two 2026 rows are labeled Statewide. The 2027 supplement says reduced-fee DAV tags are limited to 500 deer tags and 300 elk tags, that DAV deer tags are valid statewide, and that a DAV elk tag is valid in the selected zone. The 2027 DAV codes are choices, not results.
  • 2027 has no draw result. The Nonresident Supplement, published for the drawing that opens December 5, 2026, says the 2027 seasons were not set yet. Tag numbers in that supplement are based on the 2026 seasons. Final 2027 seasons are set in March 2027, and the 2027 seasons booklet is published in April 2027. A tag limit on a 2027 card is how many nonresident tags are offered. It is not a success rate. There is no 2027 band.
  • 2027 dates IDFG has published. First application for the nonresident and DAV draws: December 5–15, 2026. Results: early January 2027. Claim deadline except DAV: January 20, 2027. Second application, nonresident draw only: February 5–15, 2027. Second results: early March 2027. Final claim deadline, including DAV: March 20, 2027. There is one DAV application period, December 5–15. A person who draws in the first period cannot apply in the second, even if the tag is not claimed.
  • Who has to draw in 2027. The supplement says an applicant needs a valid Idaho hunting license. The application fee is $18 per application. Up to five hunt choices are allowed, except the DAV deer drawing has two choices, regular deer or white-tailed deer. A group can be up to four people. Drawing a general tag does not block a controlled-hunt application. Nonresident lifetime license holders do not enter this draw. They buy general tags from the resident quota in late June or early July. Junior mentored hunters draw only if they want a capped elk zone. Their deer tags and uncapped elk tags are bought over the counter in late March, after the draw, and are not part of these bands.
