Idaho draw rules this page uses

  • This is history, not your future chance. A band is the share of first-choice residents, or first-choice nonresidents, who were drawn in 2026. IDFG's own hunt page says past odds vary with the number of people who apply and only give a general idea of interest. Quotas and demand change. Drawing a tag next time is not promised.
  • No preference points and no bonus points. The current IDAPA 13.01.04 licensing rules, retrieved November 8, 2026, do not create a point system for controlled hunts. An old IDFG workshop paper about bonus points is not a current program, and this page does not invent one. Applying in earlier years does not move your band.
  • Random drawing, first choice first. IDFG's controlled-hunt page says the tags come from a random drawing. IDAPA 13.01.04.330.08 says an application that misses its first choice goes to another choice only if that hunt is not already full. IDFG publishes a combined second-choice rate that is not split by residency, so this page does not turn second choice into a band.
  • Residents and nonresidents are separate results. The band uses the residency columns in the 2026 odds table. A resident applying in a group with a nonresident should read IDFG's current application rules before relying on the resident band. The mixed-group sentence in an older IDFG question-and-answer page could not be reopened on November 8, 2026, because the main IDFG site returned an error, so it is not stated here as current rule text.
  • Nonresident cap, as written in the current rule. IDAPA 13.01.04.315, section note July 1, 2026: in controlled hunts with 19 or fewer tags, not more than one nonresident tag; in controlled hunts other than unlimited hunts with more than 20 tags, not more than 10 percent of the tags to nonresidents. The section states those two cases and does not state a separate rule for a hunt with exactly 20 tags. Outfitter-allocated hunts, NR Draw hunts, NR DAV Draw hunts, Landowner Appreciation hunts, landowner-permission hunts and emergency depredation hunts are exempt. The 2026 first-drawing results were posted in May and June 2026, before that July 1 section date. This page does not claim those May and June results were produced under the July 1 text, and it does not recompute the cap. The band is the result IDFG published.
  • Second drawing is leftover tags, not a second choice. Unclaimed deer and elk tags from the first drawing go to a second drawing (IDAPA 13.01.04.330.03). IDFG's 2026 odds table has second-drawing rows for deer, elk, pronghorn and black bear only. Those rows are a different group of people, applying later, for the tags that remained.
  • Groups. Deer, elk and pronghorn groups are 2, 3 or 4 people who must share both choices. Moose, bighorn sheep, mountain goat and black bear groups are 2 people. If the group is larger than the tags left, it is not selected (IDAPA 13.01.04.330.05). The band is people drawn divided into people who applied first choice. It is not the chance a group application draws.
  • Waiting periods and one-species limits. IDAPA 13.01.04.320 sets eligibility after a bighorn sheep, mountain goat or moose draw, after a kill, and for the following year after an antlered-only deer, antlered-only elk or pronghorn draw, with the exceptions written in that section. A person applying for bighorn sheep, mountain goat or moose cannot also apply for most other controlled hunts that year. The exceptions include unlimited controlled hunts, a controlled black bear hunt, a controlled gray wolf hunt, and a designated depredation or extra tag hunt for deer, elk or pronghorn. Read the section before you apply. This tool does not know your history, so it cannot tell you that you are eligible.
  • Youth-only hunts. Where the 2026 hunt list names a youth-only season, the card says so. IDAPA 13.01.04.320.09 limits those applications to ages 9 through 17, with the hunt itself from age 10, and allows a person who turns 18 during the hunt to finish it. In the second application period, a person 65 or older, or a person with a disabled license, may apply for a youth-only hunt. Hunts with no 2026 season name are not labeled youth, even if an older brochure called them that.
  • Extra tag hunts. IDAPA 13.01.04.350 says a controlled hunt area with an X suffix is an extra tag hunt. Cards mark that suffix. They do not print the tag count.
  • Dates. The rule says IDFG publishes application dates on its website and in brochures. It does not put the calendar in the rule. IDFG news posts for the 2026 season set deer, elk, pronghorn, swan, fall black bear and fall turkey applications at May 1 through June 5, 2026, and the second drawing at August 5 through 15, 2026. Moose, bighorn sheep and mountain goat applications were posted as April 1 through 30, 2026. Those windows are closed. The main IDFG site returned an error on November 8, 2026, so confirm those posts. This page does not state 2027 dates.
  • Not on this page. The separate nonresident general-season deer and elk tag drawing (NR Draw and NR DAV Draw, IDAPA 13.01.04.340) is not in the controlled-hunt odds table, so it has no band here. Swan and sandhill crane had no 2026 rows in that table. Gray wolf and grizzly bear are not in the odds-table species list used here. General-season over-the-counter tags are not controlled hunts. Harvest success is not on this page. Moose, both bighorn sheep, mountain goat, black bear and turkey have 2026 draw rows, and the hunt list had no 2026 season dates for them, so those cards omit dates, weapon and sex.

How a band is figured. IDFG publishes first-choice residents drawn and residents who applied, and the same pair for nonresidents. The band uses those counts. Every person drawn is "All drew." At least three out of four is "Most drew." At least one out of four is "Some drew." At least one out of twenty is "Few drew." Below that, the hunt stays off the list. The top band is only a complete draw. A whole-number rate IDFG prints can sit on the other side of a line, including a rate printed as a complete draw when the counts are not complete. Fifty-eight residency rows in this build were in that rounding gap. The exact rates are not shown on this free page. The full list is the Full Pass.
