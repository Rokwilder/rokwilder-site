Privacy

These are Rokwilder Scouting's own privacy rules, written in plain English. They are not legal advice. Nothing is for sale yet and checkout is not open. Items marked DRAFT are choices still being confirmed. Last updated November 3, 2026.

Short version: we collect only what we need to run the site and deliver what you buy, we never sell it, and we delete it when you ask, except records the law makes us keep.

Who we are

Rokwilder Scouting LLC is a family-owned Colorado company. UnitIQ is our map and data product.

What we collect today

Waitlist: only what you type into the waitlist form: your email (required), your first name (optional), the state you hunt, what you're interested in (draw odds, maps, scouting packages), and the date you signed up.

Feedback form: your message, what it's about, the page you were on (and, from Find Your Hunt, the hunt and filters you were looking at), and your email only if you choose to give it. Your IP address is kept only as a one-way hashed value, used to stop spam.

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

When accounts and checkout open

  • Account: your email, used to send you login codes and to know what you bought. We set one cookie only to keep you logged in.
  • Payments: Stripe handles payments. We don't see or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us what we need for the order, such as your name, email, billing country and ZIP code, card type and last 4 digits, and the IP address used at checkout.
  • Orders and delivery: what you bought, when, the unit and species, when we delivered it and, where our system records it, when you downloaded it. We use these records to deliver what you bought, stop fraud, and answer payment disputes.
  • Boots-on-the-Ground requests: your name, email, phone (optional), and your hunt details.

Emails we send

We'll email you receipts, deliveries, login codes, renewal reminders 25 to 40 days before each Full Pass renewal, and important changes to our terms. These aren't marketing. We send news about Rokwilder Scouting and UnitIQ only if you joined the waitlist or asked for it, and you can unsubscribe from any of those emails.

We never sell it

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. Only Christopher, the owner, can see it. Companies that help us run the site handle it only to do that job: GitHub (hosting the public site), Cloudflare (the waitlist, the feedback form and member accounts), Stripe (payments) and DRAFTthe email service we use to send email.

How long we keep it

Waitlist and feedback info stays until you ask us to delete it. DRAFTOrder and payment records are kept for 7 years for taxes and disputes, then deleted.

Deleting your info

Want off the list or want your info deleted? Email support@rokwilder.com or reply to any email from us, and we'll delete it, except order and payment records we have to keep. You can also unsubscribe from any marketing email.

Kids

The site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian has to make any purchase.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If it's a big change, we'll email account holders.

Contact

Questions about your info? Email support@rokwilder.com. See also our terms of use and refund policy.
