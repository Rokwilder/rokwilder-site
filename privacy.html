Privacy

Plain-language draft, not legal advice. This page was written in plain English to explain how we work. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet and may change. Last updated October 24, 2026.

Short version: we only collect waitlist info and feedback you send us, we never sell it, and we delete it whenever you ask.

Who we are

Rokwilder Scouting is run by Christopher in Colorado. UnitIQ is our map and data product.

What we collect

Only what you type into the waitlist form: your email (required), your first name (optional), the state you hunt, what you're interested in (draw odds, maps, scouting packages), and the date you signed up. We don't ask for anything else, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Feedback form

If you use the feedback form, we store your message, what it's about, the page you were on (and, from Find Your Hunt, the hunt and filters you were looking at), and your email only if you choose to give it. We use this to improve the site and, if you left an email, to reply. Your IP address is kept only as a one-way hashed value, used to stop spam. You can ask us to delete your feedback at christopher@rokwilder.com.

How we use it

To email you about Rokwilder Scouting and UnitIQ, like when packages or your state open up. That's it.

We never sell it

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. It's stored on Cloudflare, the service that runs our signup form, and only Christopher can see the list.

Deleting your info

Want off the list or want your info deleted? Email christopher@rokwilder.com or reply to any email from us, and we'll delete it. You can also unsubscribe from any email.

Contact

Questions about your info? Email christopher@rokwilder.com.

Kids

The waitlist is for adults. Don't sign up if you're under 13.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
