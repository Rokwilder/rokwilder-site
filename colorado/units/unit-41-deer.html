Unit 41 Deer Draw Odds, Colorado

Every limited deer hunt code in Unit 41, with a verdict for residents and out-of-state hunters at 0, 2, 5 and 10 preference points.

Unit 41 has 12 limited deer hunt codes in our data. At 0 preference points, 1 come out Likely or Yes for Colorado residents and 2 for out-of-state hunters. None is worse than Maybe for residents at 10 points. Over-the-counter licenses aren't drawn, so they aren't listed here; check the CPW brochure for over-the-counter options in this unit.

Questions

Can I draw a deer tag in Unit 41 with 0 points?

At 0 preference points, 1 of 12 hunt codes here come out Likely or Yes for Colorado residents and 2 for nonresidents. These are estimates for 2027 from CPW's past first-choice draws. Check each hunt code above.

Where do these verdicts come from?

From the draw recap reports Colorado Parks and Wildlife publishes after each primary draw. We turn past results into a verdict word for each point level. Rokwilder Scouting is not affiliated with CPW.

Can I see the exact odds?

Yes. Exact odds at every point level, with 3-year trends, are in the UnitIQ Full Pass. The verdict words here are free.
