Every limited pronghorn hunt code in Unit 115, with a verdict for residents and out-of-state hunters at 0, 2, 5 and 10 preference points.

Questions

Can I draw a pronghorn tag in Unit 115 with 0 points?

At 0 preference points, 4 of 5 hunt codes here come out Likely or Yes for Colorado residents and 4 for nonresidents. These are estimates for 2027 from CPW's past first-choice draws. Check each hunt code above.

Where do these verdicts come from?

From the draw recap reports Colorado Parks and Wildlife publishes after each primary draw. We turn past results into a verdict word for each point level. Rokwilder Scouting is not affiliated with CPW.

Can I see the exact odds?

Yes. Exact odds at every point level, with 3-year trends, are in the UnitIQ Full Pass. The verdict words here are free.
