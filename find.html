3. What weapon?

Turkey licenses aren't split by weapon: every CPW turkey hunt code ends in R, and one license covers shotgun, bow or crossbow. Rifles and handguns are legal in fall and late seasons only, not spring (CPW 2026 turkey brochure).

More optionsIs the hunter a kid (12–17)?
Youth draw preference: kids 12–17 who have hunter education get first shot at a minimum of 15% of limited licenses for doe pronghorn, antlerless and either-sex deer, and antlerless elk in each unit (Ranching for Wildlife and Air Force Academy hunts excluded). The hunter must be 17 or younger when applying, and if more than one hunt code is listed, every choice must be youth-eligible or youth-only. Check the CPW big game brochure (2026, page 17).
