Everything in one place

Draw odds, unit maps, and scouting intel in one place, built for what happens after you draw. Look up past draw results for a hunt code, see the unit's land, roads and water, and get ranked, estimated zones for your species and season, so you know where to start looking once you're in the unit.

How it works

1. Pick your unit and season

Choose a GMU, species, and archery, muzzleloader or rifle season.

2. We run the public data

Wildlife range and migration layers, water, terrain, vegetation, land ownership and roads, combined into a ranked map of estimated zones.

3. You go scout

Load the pins into onX or Gaia, check access, and put boots on the ground. The map gets you started. Your legs do the rest.
