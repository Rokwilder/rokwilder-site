How these lists differ

  • A general license is not a draw and not a harvest rate. Residents buy a general deer license and a general elk license over the counter. The rows are the 2026 dates printed on that district's regulation line. The regulation names which date is archery, general, or a shoulder season. This page does not guess the column when the line did not label it.
  • A permit is a different thing. Some districts also have a limited elk or deer permit. That permit is on the Limited permits button. The 2026 regulations say a general license may still be used in an open district outside the dates on the permit.
  • Some B licenses are over the counter. The regulations say purchase of over-the-counter deer B and elk B licenses begins June 15. A row is listed here when its regulation line says June 15 or OTC. A B license with a June 1 deadline is the drawing, on the Limited permits button. This page does not say whether a license is still for sale.
  • Nonresidents do not buy the general deer or elk license over the counter. The 2026 regulations say most nonresidents need a combination license: a Big Game Combination (17,000 issued) or a Deer Combination (4,600 issued). Those quotas are from the regulations. The preference-point report does not restate them.
  • Preference points are not bonus points. They apply only to nonresident combination licenses. The maximum is 3. Montana law, cited in the 2026 regulations as MCA 87-2-115, awards 75 percent of combination licenses in order of who has the most preference points. The other 25 percent go at random to applicants who have not purchased any preference points. Because of that random quarter, zero points can draw when one point does not.
  • 2026 Big Game and Elk combination, primary drawing. People with 0 preference points drew. People with 1 preference point did not. The 2026 report puts 0.2 to 1.8 preference points, including one whole point and party averages in that range, at no successful draws. Two points drew for many applicants. 2.2 to 3 points all drew. These are the primary drawing only. Licenses later returned and reissued are not in the report. The exact shares are in the Full Pass.
  • Deer combination is a separate quota. In 2026, zero points drew, one point drew for a smaller share, and 1.2 to 3 points all drew. Do not read the elk combination result as the deer result.
  • 2027 is not published. This page does not show 2027 odds or 2027 season dates. An outfitter client may buy a second preference point for an extra fee. That license is valid only when hunting with an outfitter. It is not a separate row here. Come Home to Hunt, Nonresident Native, and landowner-sponsored licenses are not in this table.
