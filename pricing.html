Extra unit: $39. The same maps, spots, waypoints and written plan for one more unit, added to a UnitIQ Scouting Package. For a hunt code that covers more than one unit. It counts toward that extra unit's cap for the season.

Launch pricing, tentative. Founder discount is for the Full Pass only, not packages. UnitIQ Scouting Packages are limited per unit and per season, so we don't send too many hunters to the same spots. The limit follows the official tag numbers for that unit and species: 1 package for every 25 tags, at least 3 and at most 30 for that unit that season. Over-the-counter and unlimited-tag units are a flat 30 per unit per season. An extra-unit add-on counts toward the cap of that extra unit. In larger units, buyers get a varied mix of spots so the same spots aren't handed to everyone. The Full Pass does not count. If a unit sells out after you pay, we refund that purchase in full. Boots-on-the-Ground is limited to 5 to 8 total for the first season; its total depends on the unit, travel distance and number of days and is agreed in writing before you pay.
