How Nevada's draw works

  • Bonus points, then a random draw. The March 2026 big game FAQ says an applicant gets the square of their bonus points, plus one for the application. Those are random numbers. The FAQ says the numbers run from 1 to 100 million. NDOW's bonus-point page says 1 to 99 million. The lowest number is the one that competes. This is not a preference-point system. A max point level does not guarantee the tag.
  • Categories are separate. Antlered elk, antlerless elk and spike elk each have their own points. So do horns-longer and horns-shorter antelope, and desert bighorn ram and ewe. Depredation hunts and management hunts do not add a bonus point. The FAQ says an active hunting or combination license has to be on the account at the draw or the unsuccessful application does not earn the point.
  • Residents and nonresidents are separate columns. The same FAQ says Commission Policy 24 sets the split at approximately 90 percent resident and 10 percent nonresident. Points stay with you if you change residency. This page does not recompute a quota.
  • Up to five choices. The bands here are 1st choice only. A later choice is what remained after earlier choices. The FAQ's draw order, also adopted in Commission Regulation 26-01, runs Silver State, then Partners in Wildlife, then junior mule deer, then the antlered and ram group, then depredation antlered elk and management ram, then the antlerless and ewe group, then spike elk, then depredation antlerless elk. One tag per species. Drawing one elk tag makes the other elk applications ineligible.
  • Parties. A party is allowed for mule deer, antlerless elk, or antelope with horns shorter than ears. Points are averaged, rounded to the nearest whole number, then squared, plus one. If the tags left are fewer than the party, the party does not draw. A mixed-residency mule deer party also needs enough tags in each residency. The 2025 tables do not mark parties, so this page does not either.
  • 2026 dates have passed. NDOW's apply page lists guided mule deer from February 9 to March 9, with results March 20; the main draw from March 23 to May 13, with results May 29; and the second draw from June 8. That page's second-draw deadline is June 15. Commission Regulation 26-01 says the second-draw deadline was 11:00 p.m. Pacific on June 16, and results on or before June 26. First come, first served is described as early to mid-July. CR 26-01 posts 2027 spring turkey applications before 11:00 p.m. Pacific on Monday, January 25, 2027, bonus-point-only purchases before 11:00 p.m. Pacific on Monday, February 1, 2027, and results on or before Friday, February 12, 2027. It does not post a 2027 big-game main-draw deadline, and this page does not guess one.
  • Waiting periods in the March 2026 FAQ: bighorn sheep ram 10 years; bighorn ewe 2 years; black bear 5 years after a successful harvest; elk 7 years; mountain goat 10 years; antelope 3 years; mule deer none; Nelson one-horn or management ram once in a lifetime; moose once in a lifetime. The FAQ does not split antlered elk from antlerless elk in that list. This tool does not know your draw history, so it cannot say you are eligible.

Sources, read November 8, 2026: NDOW bonus-point tables, bonus-point program, March 2026 big game FAQ, Commission Regulation 26-01 and the apply and buy page. Not legal advice.

What is left out. The public file stores a word, not a count and not a percentage. Hunts under one in twenty are left off the list. There is no 2025 mule deer antlerless table on the bonus-point page (a linked file there is from 2021, and it is not used). There is no 2025 turkey table. Moose, Rocky Mountain bighorn, elk spike, antelope with horns shorter than ears, and desert ram archery have no nonresident section in the 2025 files. Elk depredation, management ram, Silver State, Partners in Wildlife, Dream Tags and Heritage tags are not in these tables. The second draw is not in these tables. The May 29, 2026 public list names people who opted in. It is not a bonus-point table, so it is not used. Mountain lion tags are sold, not drawn in this file.

How a word is chosen. For each printed bonus-point level with a 1st-choice row, every one of those 1st-choice applicants drawing is "All drew." At least three out of four is "Most drew." At least one out of four is "Some drew." At least one out of twenty is "Few drew." Below that, the hunt stays off the list. Nothing here is an exact Nevada percentage.
