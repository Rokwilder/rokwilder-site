7. Is the hunter a youth (under 18)?

Youth on. Your list adds Arizona's youth-only hunts. Youth can hunt them up to their 18th birthday, and a youth who turns 18 after opening day can finish the hunt. No one under 10 may hunt big game in Arizona; ages 10 through 13 need hunter education first (2026–27 Arizona Hunting Regulations, PDF page 24).
