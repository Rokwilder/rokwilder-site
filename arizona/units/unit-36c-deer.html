How does the Arizona draw work?

Arizona is a bonus point draw. In the bonus pass, up to one in five of each hunt's permits go to the applicants with the most bonus points (Arizona rule R12-4-114). The rest are drawn at random, and each bonus point adds one more random number. Arizona also limits how many permits can go to nonresidents. Rokwilder Scouting is not affiliated with AZGFD.
