Questions

Can I draw the Manti/San Rafael deer hunt with 0 points?

At 0 points, 0 of 5 hunt numbers here come out Likely or Yes for Utah residents and 2 for nonresidents. Estimates for 2027 from Utah DWR's 2026 first-choice draw results.

Where do these verdicts come from?

From the drawing odds reports Utah DWR publishes after each draw, plus Utah's draw rules (half the permits to the top point holders, the rest at random with bonus entries for limited-entry hunts). Rokwilder Scouting is not affiliated with Utah DWR.

Can I see the exact odds?

Yes. Exact odds at every point level are in the UnitIQ Full Pass. The verdict words here are free.
