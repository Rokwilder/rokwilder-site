Where do these verdicts come from?

From the drawing odds reports Utah DWR publishes after each draw, plus Utah's draw rules (half the permits to the top point holders, the rest at random with bonus entries for limited-entry hunts). Rokwilder Scouting is not affiliated with Utah DWR.
