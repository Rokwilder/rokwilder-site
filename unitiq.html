What's in UnitIQ

UnitIQ is the Full Pass: $29.99 a year. It helps you pick a hunt you can draw, then learn the unit once you have the tag. Built from public data and labeled for what it is: an estimate to help you plan and scout.

Exact draw odds

Odds at every point level for every hunt, with 3-year trends. The free tool shows only the verdict word; UnitIQ shows the numbers.

Draw and success finder

Enter your points and see which hunts you can draw, side by side with how many hunters went and how many filled a tag.

AI hunt advisor

Ask plain questions about a hunt and get plain answers with the source for each fact. It never makes the odds sound better than the data.

Unit map after the draw

A general map for the unit on your tag: public and private land, roads and trails, water, terrain and migration corridors, with pins for glassing spots, water and access.

What it doesn't include

Season-by-season hotspot maps, glassing-spot waypoints (GPX/KML) and a written plan come with a Hotspot Map Pack ($29) or Full Scouting Package ($99). Each package includes 1 year of UnitIQ.

What it isn't

It's not a guarantee you'll draw or see animals, and it's not a guide. It's researched information so your time goes to the most likely hunts and ground first. Always confirm property lines and road status in the field.
