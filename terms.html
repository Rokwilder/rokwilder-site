Who we are

Rokwilder Scouting is run by Christopher in Colorado. These terms cover rokwilder.com, UnitIQ, and our scouting services.

Estimates, not guarantees

Draw odds, point projections, zones, maps and stats are estimates built from public data such as Colorado Parks and Wildlife (CPW) reports. Past draw results don't promise future draws, and an estimated zone doesn't promise animals will be there. Data can be wrong or out of date. Always check CPW's official reports before you apply or hunt.

Scouting only, not guiding or outfitting

Rokwilder Scouting is not an outfitter or guide service. We sell scouting information and scouting services. We don't take anyone hunting, and buying from us doesn't guarantee a tag, an animal or a harvest.

Follow the rules

You must follow all CPW regulations and other laws, including licensing, season dates, boundaries, and access rules. A route or zone on our maps doesn't mean you have permission to be there. Confirm land ownership, closures and signs yourself, and get permission before entering private land.

Your safety

Backcountry hunting has real risks. You're responsible for your own safety, gear and decisions in the field.

Our maps, reports and site content are Rokwilder Scouting's work, licensed for the buyer's personal use only; please don't resell, share or redistribute them. CPW and other public data belong to the public sources they came from.
