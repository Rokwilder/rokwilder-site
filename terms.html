Estimates, not guarantees

Draw odds, harvest success, point projections, hotspot maps, spots and waypoints are estimates. Draw odds and harvest data come from public reports by Colorado Parks and Wildlife, the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources and the Arizona Game and Fish Department. Past results don't guarantee that you'll draw a tag or harvest an animal. Spots and waypoints are suggestions, not guarantees that animals will be there. Conditions change: weather, fire, roads, closures, land ownership and animal movement all change from year to year and day to day. Data can be wrong or out of date. Always check the state's official regulations and reports before you apply or hunt.
